# NorAuto Match — Oklahoma Competitive Growth Plan

**Prepared:** August 15, 2026  
**Market:** Oklahoma City metro first; statewide learning set second  
**Positioning:** Personal salesperson brand operating through Orr Nissan West

## Executive conclusion

Trying to beat Oklahoma's largest rooftops at inventory, paid-media spend, OEM authority, or service capacity is a losing game. NorAuto Match can beat them at the layer large stores routinely break: one accountable human, fast response, a useful short list, an actual-unit video, written context around the numbers, and follow-through after delivery.

The winning synthesis is:

> **Country-store humanity + luxury-level follow-through + big-store access.**

The primary promise remains:

> **Tell me the number. I'll find the car.**

The necessary attribution is:

> **Your direct shopping line at Orr Nissan West.**

This review does **not** validate any dealer as the audited “#1 by sales volume.” Dealer claims, inventory counts, Google ratings, Cars.com reviews, DealerRater reviews, and CarEdge quote samples measure different things. Use the list as competitive intelligence, not as publishable rankings.

## Compliance correction before growth

Approval from the GM clears the internal-permission issue. It does not turn an employed salesperson into an independent auto broker or dealer.

### Google presence

Google's own business-profile guidelines say corporate sales associates and lead-generation agents are not eligible as individual practitioners. NorAuto Match should not operate a separate “Auto broker” Google Business Profile unless the legal business model and licensing genuinely change.

Source: [Google Business Profile representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)

Use instead:

- Orr Nissan West's eligible Google Business Profile
- an approved staff/profile page on the Orr site
- the NorAuto Match organic website
- YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok brand profiles
- an approved DealerRater employee profile
- reviews on the eligible dealership profiles that naturally mention the salesperson/brand

### Oklahoma advertising identity

Oklahoma's used-vehicle regulator specifically flags salesperson advertising that omits the employing dealership. Dealer advertising must conspicuously identify the dealership. The official guidance also warns that prices in salesperson ads must match dealer pricing.

Sources:

- [Oklahoma advertisement violations guidance](https://oklahoma.gov/oumvdmhc/about-oumvdmhc/newsroom/2022/advertisement-violations.html)
- [Oklahoma new-vehicle advertising rules](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/onmvc/documents/title465-advertising-rules-09-23.pdf)
- [Dealer price advertising compliance](https://oklahoma.gov/onmvc/laws-rules-statutes/dealer-advertising-rules-compliance-information.html)

Operating rules:

1. Identify **Orr Nissan West** conspicuously on the site and vehicle advertising.
2. Show the dealer's official phone and address in addition to the direct NorAuto Match line.
3. Never imply NorAuto Match is a separate dealership or licensed brokerage.
4. Route vehicle sales, financing, paperwork, registration, and delivery through the licensed dealer.
5. Keep advertised prices synchronized with the official source.
6. Do not advertise a specific monthly payment without the required vehicle identity, availability, selling price, credit terms, and disclosures.
7. Do not advertise “guaranteed approval,” “everybody financed,” “name your payment,” or similar claims.
8. Do not pay unapproved referral fees. Oklahoma rules restrict compensated referrals across dealerships and salesperson employment relationships.

### Platform reality

- Google Vehicle Ads are intended for eligible dealers, retailers, aggregators, and OEMs—not an individual salesperson or auto broker. Run them through the approved dealership inventory and accounts.
- Meta removed organic business-page vehicle inventory listings from Marketplace. Use approved Automotive Inventory Ads or regular content/lead ads; do not create dummy personal accounts to skirt the rule.

Sources:

- [Google vehicle advertiser integration guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15786784?hl=en)
- [Meta Marketplace dealer change overview](https://autorevo.com/facebook-marketplace-is-gone-for-auto-dealers-now-what/)

## What NorAuto Match should own

### Category

Do not try to become “the largest dealer.” Become:

> **The most trusted direct car-shopping contact in the northwest Oklahoma City metro.**

That is measurable and believable.

### The NorAuto Standard

Every direct lead should receive the same operating experience:

1. **One accountable person** — first text through delivery and after-sale questions.
2. **A real match brief** — budget, use case, must-haves, and three realistic options.
3. **Actual-unit video** — condition, key features, tires, interior, cargo, and visible flaws before the drive.
4. **Numbers with context** — vehicle selling price, dealer/doc fee, estimated TTL, down/trade assumption, term, APR assumption, optional products, and what remains unconfirmed.
5. **Appointment readiness** — exact unit verified and staged before the customer leaves home.
6. **Delivery orientation** — pair phone, set driver preferences, explain controls, schedule first service.
7. **Follow-through** — delivery-day message, 72-hour check-in, 30-day check-in, and service handoff.

Do not promise a guaranteed OTD number before the necessary trade, credit, tax-jurisdiction, and product variables are known. Promise a clear written recap of what is known, assumed, and still open.

### Memorable ritual

Carter Chevrolet has the chicken-dinner story. NorAuto needs its own repeatable ritual:

**The NorAuto Delivery Brief**

- personalized delivery photo/video
- one-page “what you bought” summary
- paired phone and safety settings
- first-service date placed on calendar
- direct-line contact card
- 72-hour follow-up

It is inexpensive, photographable, review-worthy, and operationally useful.

## Acquisition system

### Phase 1: proof before paid reach — days 1–30

- Publish five short-form videos per week.
- Respond to every direct lead during posted hours with a target median under 10 minutes.
- Produce one actual-unit video for every qualified appointment.
- Request an honest review from every eligible delivered buyer—no gating and no scripted rating.
- Record source, first-response time, appointment, show, sale, gross, and review outcome in CRM.

Content franchises:

- **Payment Match Monday** — one budget, three realistic vehicle types, complete assumptions
- **Deal Decoder** — explain price, TTL, trade equity, term, and APR
- **Truck Fit Friday** — towing/payload/commute/family use case
- **Video Before the Drive** — exact-unit walkaround
- **What I Would Buy** — honest recommendation for a specific buyer job
- **Delivery Proof** — customer-approved delivery story

### Phase 2: high-intent search — days 31–60

Run through the approved dealership advertising structure.

Initial keyword lane:

- cars by monthly payment Oklahoma City
- car finder Oklahoma City
- personal car shopper OKC
- cars under $500 a month OKC
- help finding a car Oklahoma City
- family SUV Mustang OK
- truck finder Yukon OK
- car delivery Piedmont OK

Do not bid competitor dealer names in phase one. It is expensive, invites internal/channel conflict, and catches navigational traffic that already chose a store.

### Phase 3: inventory media — days 61–90

After the feed is accurate:

- connect approved dealer inventory to Google Merchant Center
- run eligible Vehicle Ads through the dealership account
- run Meta Automotive Inventory Ads through approved dealer assets
- pass UTM, campaign, vehicle, source, and salesperson attribution into CRM
- upload offline sold outcomes back to ad platforms without sending prohibited PII

1. New lead
2. Contact attempted
3. Connected
4. Match brief complete
5. Options sent

7. Appointment set
8. Appointment confirmed
9. Showed
10. Desking / trade / credit
11. Sold
12. Delivered
13. 72-hour follow-up
14. 30-day follow-up
15. Service handoff
16. Nurture
17. Lost with reason

### Required fields

- lead source and UTM data
- city
- target payment
- cash/down/trade equity
- term preference
- body style and must-haves
- purchase timing
- contact preference
- vehicle/VIN interest
- appointment and show status
- lost reason
- sold vehicle and date
- review requested / received

### Automation

- immediate confirmation text/email
- owner alert at 5 minutes
- escalation at 15 minutes during posted hours
- appointment confirmation at booking, 24 hours, and 2 hours
- no-show recovery after 15 minutes and next morning
- 72-hour and 30-day owner follow-up
- 9-, 18-, and 30-month equity/service nurture where permitted

## What not to do

- Do not publish “#1 salesperson” until an auditable metric and period support it.
- Do not create a fake Auto Broker Google Business Profile.
- Do not omit Orr Nissan West from vehicle advertising.
- Do not use competing dealer names in domains, metadata, or misleading ads.
- Do not scrape customer lists, impersonate stores, or solicit another dealer's protected leads.
- Do not attack competitors using isolated negative reviews.
- Do not post dummy Marketplace listings or down-payment-only prices.
- Do not show stale or fictional inventory as live.
- Do not publish a payment without complete approved disclosures.
- Do not buy broad “car” traffic.

The play is not dirtier marketing. It is a cleaner operating system.
