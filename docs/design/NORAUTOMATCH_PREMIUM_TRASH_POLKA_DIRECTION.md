# NorAuto Match — Premium Automotive × Controlled Trash Polka Direction

## Objective
Create a distinctive, memorable automotive shopping experience without turning the site into visual chaos. The design should feel premium, modern, slightly rebellious, and unmistakably NorAuto Match.

Core brand:
- NORauto Match
- CAR SHOPPING, WITHOUT THE PRESSURE
- Find the car that fits the person first.

## Visual Formula
Use a premium automotive base with controlled Trash Polka influence.

Palette:
- 80% deep navy / near-black / warm white
- 10% amber-gold for money, value, selected states, rational information
- 10% crimson-red for emotion, discovery, preference, comparison, emphasis
- Emerald only for verified / confirmed positive truth states

Trash Polka influence should be interpreted through:
- bold asymmetry
- red brush strokes / slashes
- oversized cropped typography
- subtle halftone / distressed texture
- stencil-style numerals
- thin diagrammatic lines
- collage energy
- controlled imperfection

Do not make the site look tattoo-themed. Borrow the visual grammar, not the literal subject matter.

Hard rule: never use more than one major expressive graphic device in the same viewport.

## Visual Rhythm
Use alternating intensity so the page does not become exhausting:

RAW → CLEAN → RAW → CLEAN → RAW → CALM → TRUSTED → RAW

Suggested journey:
1. Hero — expressive
2. SwipeMatch — clean interaction
3. Match DNA — expressive personalization
4. Garage — clean shortlist
5. Garage Battle — expressive comparison
6. Money — calm and trustworthy
7. Verified Inventory — clean and factual
8. Final CTA — expressive close

## Mobile Experience
Design mobile first around 390 × 844, also supporting 360 / 412 / 430 widths.

### Hero
Headline:
TELL ME
THE NUMBER.

Use a bold crimson brush strike or graphic mark behind / through “NUMBER.”

Support copy should be short and human.

Possible line:
Your budget. Your life. Your match.

Primary CTA:
START MY MATCH

Secondary:
BROWSE CARS

One strong cinematic vehicle image. No stacked trust-card wall under the hero.

### SwipeMatch
Keep the actual interaction visually clean.

One dominant vehicle card with strong imagery and small useful data.

Controls:
PASS / MAYBE / LIKE

Gesture support plus accessible buttons.

A LIKE can briefly trigger a restrained red “KEEP” slash/stamp interaction. No confetti or dating-app parody.

### Match DNA
Make this the signature payoff.

Use large cropped type:
YOUR
MATCH
DNA

Surround it with preference signals such as:
- AWD — STRONG SIGNAL
- MIDSIZE SUV — 5 / 7 likes
- CARGO — HIGH PRIORITY
- PERFORMANCE — MEDIUM
- PAYMENT — < $550 TARGET

Some signals can be clean amber chips; others can use red stamp/annotation treatment. Thin diagram lines and a faint vehicle blueprint/silhouette can support the composition.

Then explain in plain English what NorAuto Match learned and surface 3 recommended vehicle families.

### My Garage
Heading:
YOUR GARAGE

Optional red handwritten annotation:
3 survivors

Cards should be cleaner than the surrounding expressive graphics.

Each vehicle:
- image
- model
- match reason / score
- compare
- remove

Primary action:
START A GARAGE BATTLE

Horizontal rails should use deliberate snap and peek behavior. Never clip the first card.

### Garage Battle
Make comparison a second visual peak.

Example:
ROGUE
VS
CR-V

Use one strong red VS slash / divider.

Lead with “For You” rather than a universal winner.

Explain where each vehicle wins based on the shopper’s observed priorities.

Possible dimensions:
- family fit
- value
- fuel cost
- cargo
- technology
- performance
- ownership cost
- reliability evidence
- verified availability when real

Technical specifications should be secondary / expandable.

### Money
Deliberately calm the design here.

Heading:
WHAT NUMBER FEELS RIGHT?

Controls may include:
- target monthly payment
- down payment
- trade equity
- term

Use amber for controls and values.

Frame all results as estimates / buying lanes unless real finance data proves stronger claims.

Show recommendation narrowing visually, e.g.:
8 vehicles → 4 realistic matches

### Verified Inventory
Keep this section factual and premium.

Heading:
MATCHES YOU CAN ACTUALLY SHOP

No Trash Polka theatrics here.

If nothing is currently verified:
No verified match right now.
Your preferences are saved. We can keep searching without showing you stale inventory.

Actions:
KEEP SEARCHING
FIND ONE FOR ME

Never fake availability.

### Final CTA
Bring the attitude back.

Possible headline:
STOP SHOPPING
14 TABS.

Supporting line:
Tell NorAuto what matters. Let the noise die.

CTA:
FIND MY MATCH

Use one strong automotive image and one red expressive mark.

## Desktop Experience
Use the same language at 1440px but make it more cinematic and editorial.

- Hero: split composition / large vehicle imagery / oversized cropped type
- SwipeMatch: large central card with contextual side information
- Match DNA: broad visual dossier / preference constellation
- Garage: wide shelf of vehicles
- Garage Battle: magazine-like split-screen comparison
- Money: controls on one side, recommendation reaction on the other
- Inventory: clean premium grid

Do not simply stretch mobile.

## UX Principles
- Utility before contact capture
- Interaction early
- Important information cannot be buried
- Reduce simultaneous cards / borders / badges
- Use whitespace as a design tool
- Every viewport needs a clear visual priority
- The user should always know:
  1. Where am I?
  2. What did NorAuto Match learn?
  3. What should I do next?

## Motion
Good:
- restrained card swipes
- red KEEP stamp / slash
- Match DNA signals appearing
- Garage cards sliding into place
- comparison emphasis changing
- soft verified-state glow

Avoid:
- confetti
- slot-machine effects
- constant pulsing
- heavy parallax
- excessive motion

## Brand / Truth Boundary
Do not expose the inventory-source dealership in normal customer-facing discovery UX.

Use:
- Verified Inventory
- Verified Match
- Available Matches
- Fresh inventory is being verified

Do not invent:
- live inventory updated in real time
- vehicle available now
- reserved for you
- dealer approved
- exact financing approval

The backend will preserve exact provenance and reveal source identity only when necessary for the transaction, visit, legal disclosure, or customer-authorized next step.

## Desired Impression
The result should not feel like “dark SaaS with car photos.”

It should feel like:
- premium automotive editorial
- smart personal buying assistant
- trustworthy utility
- controlled rebellion
- a car-shopping brand with an actual point of view

The ideal reaction is:
“What car website is THAT?”
