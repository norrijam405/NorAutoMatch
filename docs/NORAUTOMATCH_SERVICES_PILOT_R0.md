# NorAutoMatch Services — pilot commercial scope R0
**2026-11-17 | Draft / nonproduction / no authorized public offer**

## Positioning
An automotive customer-experience service company with two audiences:
- **Dealer managers:** read-only public website/inventory UX and shopper-handoff audit; later experimental Torque pilot.
- **Individual sales professionals:** optional content/landing-page process consulting that respects their employer's policies, dealer trademarks and lead ownership.

This is not a licensed-dealer storefront, vehicle brokerage authorization, customer referral commission arrangement or financial-services offer. The existing Orr integration remains a permitted technical experiment subject to confirming current permission scope for commercial use after employment changes.

## Three initial packages, no quoted price until central Cost Layer 2.0 integration
1. Digital Checkup: public website review -> findings report -> reproduction details -> prioritized fixes. **First to sell**. No authenticated data.
2. Salesperson Online Presence: approved content workflow/landing-page scope -> sample deliverables -> explicit dealer permission -> client approval; no duplicated CRM or lead capture.
3. Torque Evaluation Pilot: fixed-duration demo using fictional or expressly authorized data -> acceptance report and gap register. R28 branch tests prove selected synthetic behavior, **not** complete multi-dealer deployment or full browser acceptance.

## Offer-to-cash operating workflow
Inquiry via customer-initiated email -> manual qualification -> permission and compliance review -> quote with ONE House pricing floor and real time/hosting/support estimates -> explicit acceptance and written agreement -> scoped work -> evidence-reviewed delivery -> manual invoice through an approved existing system. No automatic follow-up, price computation engine, Stripe charge, customer-data transfer or referral fee.

## Cost Layer 2.0 input contract to request (not implemented)
- Quote line items: task, estimated hours, verified hourly cost, one-time third-party charges, recurring provider costs, support reserve, contingency, taxes/fees as applicable.
- Policies: floor version and currency, approval actor, eligible friend/family discount bounded by floor, expiration, exception approval evidence.
- Quote status: PROPOSED / FLOOR_CHECK_PENDING / APPROVED / REJECTED; never claim enforceable before a verified House runtime contract exists.
- Dealer vehicle listing prices are dealer-sourced evidence and **not** service-package quote prices. Keep the two distinct.

## Acceptance gates before taking money or public promotion
- Document actual legal entity and contract owner; check state service/broker and advertising rules; compensation arrangements involving vehicle sales receive specialist review.
- Confirm any dealer logos, customer data, named affiliation, media assets or VIN feed rights **in writing for commercial scope**. A previous development approval does not by itself prove current resale/sublicense rights.
- Present example audits as demonstrations until completed with permission and quality review. Never promise increased sales, stock accuracy or automated appointments.
- Obtain client signoff on scope, deliverable, price, timeframe, access, rights, data retention and support.
- Review security boundaries and keep intake email-only until a properly governed consent/privacy process exists.

## Minimum first paid-project experiment
1. Produce one *fictional or publicly sourced* sample website audit with anonymized findings and no dealership endorsement.

3. Price via central Cost Layer 2.0; if central floor not connected, quote review is pending—not bypassed.
